# Research Synthesis Skill

Use this skill when the user requests in-depth research, competitive analysis, technology comparison, literature review, or fact-checking on any domain.

---

## 1. Core Principles

1. **Objective & Evidence-Based**: Ground findings in factual data, primary documentation, and cross-verified sources.
2. **Balanced Perspectives**: Acknowledge trade-offs, alternative approaches, and dissenting evidence rather than presenting a one-sided view.
3. **Structured & Scannable**: Format outputs with clear headings, bullet points, and comparison matrices for quick comprehension.
4. **Transparent Uncertainty**: Clearly distinguish between verified facts, working hypotheses, and open questions.

---

## 2. Research Workflow

### Phase 1: Plan & Scope
1. **Clarify the Core Question**: Define the precise research problem, target audience, and depth required.
2. **Identify Key Dimensions**: Break down the topic into sub-questions (e.g., feasibility, cost, performance, security, alternatives).
3. **Determine Sources**: Identify authoritative reference points (official documentation, benchmarks, whitepapers, specifications).

### Phase 2: Propose the Framework
1. **Outline the Investigation**: Share the proposed research outline and sub-topics with the user.
2. **Confirm Scope**: Ensure the focus aligns with the user's expectations before conducting extensive investigation.

### Phase 3: Gather & Synthesize
1. **Information Gathering**: Search, read, and extract key insights from relevant materials.
2. **Cross-Referencing**: Corroborate claims across multiple independent references. Discard unverified speculation.
3. **Structured Synthesis**: Organize findings into thematic sections:
   - **Executive Summary**: 2–3 sentence high-level takeaway.
   - **Comparative Findings**: Side-by-side analysis (pros/cons, metrics, feature tables).
   - **Risks & Trade-offs**: Nuances, limitations, or potential blind spots.
   - **Actionable Recommendations**: Clear, practical next steps based on the evidence.

### Phase 4: Verify & Fact-Check
1. **Source Validation**: Verify that quotes, figures, and technical claims accurately reflect their sources.
2. **Logical Consistency**: Check that recommendations logically follow from the presented evidence.
3. **Clarity Review**: Eliminate jargon where plain language works better; ensure concise formatting.

---

## 3. Deliverable Templates

When delivering research reports, prefer standard scannable structures:

- **Executive Brief**: Summary + key metrics + decision table.
- **Deep-Dive Technical Review**: Architecture overview + benchmark comparison + implementation recommendations.
- **Market / Competitive Analysis**: Market landscape + player comparison matrix + strategic implications.
